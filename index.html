02 / A little about me

Created with intention, an observant eye, and hands-on experimentation.

I’m Cassidy, an independent graphic designer and illustrator drawn to the quieter side of things. My practice moves between digital illustration and visual identity.

My work explores the theme of androgyny because femininity and masculinity in combination are beautiful in my eyes. I want to challenge my viewers’ eyes and let them determine what they see, be fluid and open to their own interpretation. I often choose monochromatic palettes because I do not want color to shift the focus from the figures, their expressions, gestures, or presence. My goal is to create work that is personal to me while allowing viewers to connect in their own way.
